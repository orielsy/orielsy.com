# Architectural Decision Record (ADR) 001: Domain & Brand Relationship (orielsy.com & bodyctrl.com)

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-26
- **Updated:** 2026-09-13
- **Context:** Orielsy Diaz owns two domains: `orielsy.com` and `bodyctrl.com`.

---

## Decision

1. **orielsy.com** is Orielsy's canonical personal home on the web, representing his professional identity, technical research, architecture explorations, software projects, experiments, and emerging interests.
2. **bodyctrl.com** is preserved as an intentional, distinct identity and possible future creative frontend home.
   - The name represents `<body> + Ctrl`: the craft of controlling what users experience inside the document body, alongside physical/digital control systems.
   - Do **NOT** treat BODYCTRL as an obsolete portfolio or automatically redirect it to `orielsy.com`.
   - Do **NOT** describe BODYCTRL as an already established, fully operational standalone lab; its role remains intentionally open-ended.
3. **For now, public experimentation is surfaced only on `orielsy.com`.**
   - Do not promote or link `bodyctrl.com` as an active experimentation destination from `orielsy.com` while BODYCTRL is inactive.
   - Do not label BODYCTRL as the current lab, experimentation home, or active creative surface.
   - Any future reintroduction of visible cross-links to BODYCTRL requires an explicit decision that the site is active enough to receive public traffic.
4. Content is not duplicated across domains. `orielsy.com` receives primary publishing and experimentation for the current phase.
